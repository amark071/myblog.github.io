Profile

I am a master's student in Big Data at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University. I received my B.S. in Information and Computational Science, with a focus on Computational Mathematics, from Peking University in 2025.

My work sits at the intersection of numerical linear algebra, high-performance computing, and AI systems. I have research and engineering experience in streaming algorithms, sparse matrix solvers, large language model inference optimization, mixed-precision deep learning, and parallel programming.
